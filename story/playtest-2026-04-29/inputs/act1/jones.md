# Jones Junket — Act 1 Briefing: The Flood

---

## Story Brief

You are the Jones Junket — scholars, disciples, and lifelong devotees of the legendary Indiana Jones. You have spent years at his seminars, absorbing every word, preparing for this very moment: your maiden expedition. Three months of painstaking schematic analysis revealed a secret entrance into the temple's lower level. Three months! And worth every second.

If you succeed here, the great Indiana Jones will finally retire, and your careers begin. No pressure.

You entered through the hidden passage to find you are not alone. Across the chamber, a crew in tactical gear and balaclavas is inspecting the walls near a blast hole — the Drake Delegation, apparently. They used *dynamite*. Near the far wall, a team in jorts and styled hair is pulling at the stone — the Croft Company. Neither group appears to have brought pencils. The water is rising — and given the blast hole in the wall, it's not hard to guess why.

Your notes didn't mention flooding. Adapt.

---

## House Identity

Jones Junket are academic disciples on their maiden expedition, eager, idealistic, and meticulous. They prepared for years. Their instinct is scholarly and reverential — read the walls, decode the language, treat every inscription as a signpost. They carry pencils. They take notes. They believe in getting it right over getting it fast.

### Mission 1: Retrieve Your Ceremonial Whips

**Story blurb:** In the rush through the hidden passage, your ceremonial whips were left behind — and the floodwater is swallowing the way back. The passage branches into half-submerged corridors, each with a QRian word carved above. Read the wall right and it tells you which corridor is safe. One swimmer, one breath. Translation fragments are scattered — some yours, some pocketed by the others…

**Puzzle:** The corridor entrances are labeled with QRian glyphs:

A wall inscription reads:

{{{IT EATS ALL STONE NONE CAN SEE IT}}}

Enter the corridor name.

### Mission 2: Activate the Ancient Drainage

**Story blurb:** Along the lower walls, half-obscured by mineral deposits — a 3×3 grid of carved pipe sections, an engineered drainage network. Source and drain are visible but seven sections are missing, their Channel Stones scattered. Everything Jones came here to read is upstairs — and getting wet. You didn't spend three months on schematics for nothing. The other teams pocketed some — you'll want them back…

**Puzzle:** A 3×3 pipe grid. SOURCE (top-left, opens Right) and DRAIN (bottom-right, opens Left) are fixed. Seven pipe sections must be placed so water flows through ALL 9 cells in one continuous path. Each section has specific openings (Left, Right, Top, Bottom), and one valve is sabotaged — still part of the path, but broken.

The pipe sections were dislodged by the flood and scattered across the chamber — some in your hands, others pocketed by the houses around you. Gather all three *Pipe Bundle* clue cards from the chamber to learn each section's openings and identify the sabotaged valve.

Enter the valve numbers in flow order, skipping the sabotaged one.

**Required clue sets:** Pipe Bundle × 3

**Format:** Single text answer — five digits representing valve numbers in flow order (the sabotaged valve is excluded)

### Mission 3: Sort the Scattered Offerings

**Story blurb:** High on the far wall — three deep alcoves, each stained a different color from centuries of offerings. Six stone vessels lie on the floor below, knocked loose by the flood. Each holds a residue and carries a letter. Read the alcoves right and they'll tell you what this place actually is. Some vessels are with you; the others have the rest…

**Puzzle:** Three alcoves are stained from centuries of use:

- **Left alcove:** stained deep BLUE
- **Center alcove:** stained AMBER-GOLD
- **Right alcove:** stained DARK GREEN

Six numbered vessels lie below — each has an interior residue and a letter carved underneath. The flood scattered them across the chamber floor; some are in your hands, others picked up by the houses around you.

Gather all three *Sealed Stone Vessel* clue cards from the chamber to read each vessel's residue and letter. Then match each vessel to the alcove whose stain its residue connects to, and read the letters in order: left alcove to right, lower-numbered vessel first within each pair. The letters will reveal what this place truly was.

**Required clue sets:** Sealed Stone Vessel × 3

**Format:** Single text answer — one English word spelled by the letters in alcove order

### Mission 5: Map the False Exit

**Story blurb:** Off to the left, beyond a low archway — a corridor that tilts *up*. After a temple where everything descends, an upward slope feels like sunlight. It looks like a way out. Three months of schematics say: don't trust it. The carved waymarkers fell long ago — some sit on your table, others were pocketed by the houses around you…

**Puzzle:** Seven carved waymarkers once lined the corridor walls. They fell long ago, and the houses around you pocketed them off the chamber floor as worn old stones — numbered 1–7 in the catalogue, but the numbers are just labels. They do not indicate the path order.

Gather all three *Worn Stone Marker* clue cards from the chamber, read the seven markers, then arrange them in the order a person walking through the passage from the archway inward would encounter them.

Enter the seven marker numbers as a sequence.

**Required clue sets:** Worn Stone Marker × 3

**Format:** Single text answer — seven digits in path order

## Glyph System

The text below explains the QRian writing system you will encounter. Glyph notation `{{{WORD}}}` means the word is written in QRian glyphs — the translation key is on your clue cards.

### QRian Central Glyph System

The Central Glyph System is the primary QRian writing system used across all puzzle types. Each glyph maps to an English letter (A–Z) or digit (1–9) — 35 characters total.

**Design Principles:** All glyphs are based on a square/rectangular frame — "cube-like," matching the lore of a language shaped by the Source. Variation comes from which sides are open/closed, internal lines (horizontal/vertical), and dot placement. All roughly the same size — they line up in rows like text. Drawable by hand quickly. Share a family resemblance but are individually distinct.

**Usage:** The glyph font maps English letters to QRian glyphs — when you see {{{WORD}}} notation, the glyph sequence spells out those English letters. Your clue cards will give you the specific glyph-to-letter mappings you need to decode inscriptions.

**Multiple Glyph Systems:** While this is the Central Glyph System (letter/number substitution), other glyph modes exist: Conceptual glyphs (Type C) represent concepts/words, not letters; Mathematical glyphs (Type H) use glyphs as variables and operations; Temporal glyphs (Type L) describe time. The Central Glyph System is the foundation.
