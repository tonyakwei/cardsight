# Host Narration — Temple of the QRians

These are stage-direction notes for Anthony (the host). Read these aloud before each act begins, to set the scene on top of what each house already read on their printed story sheets.

---

## Act 1 — The Flood

Three rival expedition teams converge in the lowest basement chamber of a newly discovered temple. The architecture is strange — stone heavier than it should be, corridors that tilt at wrong angles, a faint mineral smell in the air that no one can identify. One team blasted their way in with dynamite; a second found a secret passage through three months of schematic analysis; a third tunneled up through the floor. They were expecting to be alone. None of them were.

The water is already rising — black and cold, creeping through channels in the floor that seem almost *designed* for it. Someone's entrance caused this, or the temple did. The walls are covered in a script no one has successfully deciphered. The ceiling above the central chamber is dark. And somewhere, scattered across the floor, are objects that don't belong to anyone yet.

You have time to do a few things before the water forces you up. Choose them carefully.

---

## Act 2 — The Corruption

You climbed out of the flood and into something stranger. The upper temple — the Green Department — smells of old medicine and new growth. Vines hang in three-story curtains. Glass phials line shelves that still hold liquid. The air is sweet in a way that makes the back of your throat tighten.

The temple is sealing itself. You can hear the stone doors closing, one corridor at a time, somewhere behind and below you. The clock is short and you can feel it.

This was a working floor. The QRians made things here — wrote things here — measured things here. Whatever they were doing, they did it obsessively and they documented everything. Walls, shelves, floor tiles, vines. Every surface is a record. Some of what they left behind is still here. Some of it is scattered. Some of it requires cooperation with people you don't entirely trust.

You have time to do a few things before the temple finishes sealing. Choose them carefully.

---

## Act 3 — The Dying Light

You've reached the final chamber. The doors have stopped moving. The air is very still.

This is the moment the temple was built toward — not by you, but by the QRians who designed every false exit, every flood channel, every sealed compartment. They brought everyone who entered here to this exact question. They faced it themselves, centuries ago, and they chose an answer. You've spent two acts reading the record of that choice.

Now you make your own.

This act isn't about who finds something first. It's about what twelve fragments of history add up to, and what the people in this room decide to do with that knowledge. Pool everything you've learned. Reconstruct the timeline. Then argue — respectfully, or not so respectfully — about what the world outside this temple deserves to know, and what it deserves to be protected from.

The host will record your answer when you're ready.
